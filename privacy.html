Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 June 2026

Linq is operated by Adastra Network ("we", "us", "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal data — whether you use Linq as a Member (customer) or as a Vendor (participating business). This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and share it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have.

2. Lawful bases for processing (UK & EEA users)

We rely on the following legal bases under UK GDPR:

  • Contract performance — operating your account, stamp cards, sticker system, challenges, and all features you have signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests — fraud prevention, platform security, and improving our service, balanced against your privacy rights.
  • Consent — push notifications and any optional marketing. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing.
  • Legal obligation — where we are required to retain or disclose data by law.

3. How we use your data

  • To operate loyalty stamp cards and sticker programmes
  • To run challenges, games, leaderboards, and prize draws
  • To personalise your avatar, experience, and discovery feed
  • To show you nearby stores and relevant Vendor offers
  • To power in-app messaging between Members and Vendors
  • To run Vendor dashboards and programme analytics
  • To send push notifications you have opted into
  • To detect fraud and maintain platform security
  • To improve the app and fix technical issues
  • To share anonymised, aggregated behavioural patterns with third parties (e.g. research organisations, local councils, commercial partners). Your personal details are never included — only statistical patterns at the suburb or city level.

4. Who we share your data with

  • Vendors you join — a Vendor can see activity on their own programme (your stamp and sticker progress, messages you send them). They cannot see your activity at any other business.
  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, database, file storage, push messaging, and analytics infrastructure. Firebase processes data on our behalf under its own security commitments.
  • Stripe — payment processing for Vendor add-on subscriptions. We do not store payment card details.
  • Anonymised research and commercial partners — aggregated, non-personal statistical data only.
  • Law enforcement or regulators — where required by applicable law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Linq, its users, or the public.

We do not sell personal data. We never share your personal details with advertisers.

5. Data retention

  • Account data is retained for as long as your account is active.
  • Raw behavioural event data is retained for up to 24 months, after which it is aggregated into statistical summaries and the raw records are deleted.
  • In-app messages are retained while your account is active. Deleted messages are removed within 30 days.
  • Crash and performance logs are retained for up to 90 days.
  • On account deletion, personal data is removed as described in section 6.

6. Account deletion

Members — deleting your account permanently removes your profile, display name, loyalty cards, sticker collection, pixel avatar, daily wheel history, challenge entries, messages, posts, follows, notifications, and all analytics events linked to you. Anonymised statistical aggregates (containing no identifying information) may be retained.

Vendors — deleting your business account permanently removes your business profile and every store you own, including the loyalty programme, branding, offers, automations, posts, reviews, and all Member loyalty-card and sticker data tied to that store. Members will see the card disappear from their wallet. No personal data about Members remains linked to your business afterwards. This action cannot be undone.

Delete your account any time from Settings → Delete Account in the app, or by emailing info@adastranetwork.co.uk.

7. Your rights

Wherever you are based, you can ask us to:

  • Provide a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data (subject to legal obligations)

If you are in the UK or EEA, you additionally have rights under UK/EU GDPR to:

  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Request portability of data you have provided to us
  • Withdraw consent at any time (e.g. for push notifications)
  • Lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk, or your local supervisory authority

Email info@adastranetwork.co.uk to exercise any of these rights. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards including encryption in transit (TLS), Firestore security rules, Firebase Authentication, and strict access controls to protect your data. No system is completely secure. If we become aware of a breach likely to put you at risk, we will notify you and any relevant regulator as required by law.

9. International data transfers

Your data is processed via Google Cloud / Firebase infrastructure, which may include servers outside the UK or EEA. Google applies appropriate safeguards (including Standard Contractual Clauses) consistent with UK GDPR and EU data protection law.

10. Children

Linq is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us at info@adastranetwork.co.uk and we will remove it promptly.

11. Cookies & analytics

The Linq app uses Firebase Analytics (Google) for crash reporting, performance monitoring, and usage analytics. Firebase may collect device identifiers, app version, session duration, and feature interaction events. This data is used to improve the app and is not used to identify you personally or serve advertising.

If you visit our website, standard web server logs may collect your IP address, browser type, and pages visited for security and performance purposes.

12. Push notifications

With your permission, Linq may send push notifications for stamp milestones, challenge reminders, Vendor offers, and prize draw updates. You can manage or disable notifications at any time in your device Settings or in the Linq app under Settings → Notifications.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will flag significant changes in-app or by email before they take effect. The "last updated" date above always reflects the current version. Continued use of Linq after a policy update constitutes acceptance.

Questions about this policy? Email us at info@adastranetwork.co.uk and we'll respond within 30 days.
