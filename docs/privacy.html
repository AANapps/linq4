Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Linq ("we", "us", "our") is operated by Adastra Network and is committed to protecting your personal information — whether you use Linq as a member or as a participating business ("vendor"). This policy explains what data we collect, how we use and share it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it.

Information we collect from members

Your name, email address, profile handle, profile photo, location (only when you grant permission), and your loyalty activity — stamps, points, stickers, challenge entries, posts, comments, messages, and follows. We also collect anonymised behavioural data: visit timestamps, day of week, time of day, suburb of visit, consecutive visit counts, and screen interaction patterns.

Information we collect from vendors

If you register a business, we collect your business name, registration/company number, country, contact details, store location, branding assets you upload (logos, offer artwork), and the loyalty programme you configure (stamp/point thresholds, rewards, automations, challenges). We also process activity from members who interact with your store — cards held, stamps issued, rewards redeemed, and messages exchanged — so your programme can run.

How we use it

To operate loyalty cards and reward programmes, personalise your experience, show nearby stores and challenges, power messaging, run your merchant dashboard and analytics (for vendors), and send the notifications you opt into. Anonymised, aggregated behavioural patterns may be shared with third parties such as research organisations, councils, and commercial partners (e.g. real estate firms, urban planners) — your personal details are never included, only statistical patterns.

Who we share it with

Vendors can see the activity of members on their own loyalty programme (e.g. card status, stamp/point progress, messages sent to their store) — never a member's activity at other businesses. We use Firebase (Google) for authentication, hosting, and messaging infrastructure, which processes data on our behalf under its own security and confidentiality commitments. Anonymised, aggregated insights may be shared with third-party research and commercial partners as described above. We do not sell personal data, and we never share your personal details with advertisers.

Data retention

We retain account data for as long as your account is active. Raw behavioural-event data is kept for up to 24 months, after which it is aggregated into statistical summaries and the underlying raw records are deleted. If you delete your account, your data is removed as set out below.

What happens when you delete your account

Members — deleting your account permanently removes your profile, posts, comments, follows, loyalty cards, sticker collections, messages, notifications, and analytics events linked to you.

Vendors — deleting your business account permanently removes your business profile and every store you own, including its loyalty programme, offers, automations, posts and reviews, and all member loyalty-card and sticker data tied to that store. Members simply see the card disappear from their wallet — no personal data about them remains linked to your business afterwards. This action cannot be undone. You can delete your account any time from Settings → Delete Account, or by emailing info@adastranetwork.co.uk.

Your rights

Wherever you're based, you can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. If you're in the UK or EEA you also have rights under GDPR to restrict or object to processing, request portability, and withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these rights, email info@adastranetwork.co.uk and we'll respond within 30 days.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards — encryption in transit, access controls, and Google/Firebase's security infrastructure — to protect your information. No system is fully secure, but we work hard to keep your data safe and will notify you and any relevant regulator if a breach is likely to put you at risk.

International transfers

Your data may be processed on servers outside your home country (including via Google Cloud / Firebase infrastructure). Where this happens, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

Children

Linq is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

Cookies & analytics

We use Firebase (Google) for authentication, storage, and crash/performance analytics. No personally identifiable data is shared with advertisers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll flag significant changes in-app or by email, and the "last updated" date above always reflects the latest version.
